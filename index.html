Capacity: total vs. per-client throughput

Each point is the average of all trials run with that many laptops. The total line is the access point's ceiling: it should stay roughly flat and drift down slightly as clients are added, since more clients mean more contention and management traffic eating airtime. A steep drop means that overhead is significant. The per-client line is what each laptop gets out of that ceiling.
